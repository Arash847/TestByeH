Make the dumper poll for the stub instead of connecting once

The stub binds its port partway through kstuff startup, so a single
connect attempt raced the user's reload and lost. The dumper now polls
until the port answers, so it can be started BEFORE kstuff.elf is loaded
and removes the timing pressure entirely.

Verified against a mock deliberately delayed by 6 seconds: connected,
handshaked and dumped with zero byte mismatches.
