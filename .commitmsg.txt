Revert -DINTERRUPTER_THREAD: enabling it regresses kstuff startup

Measured on the console: with -DDEBUG -DINTERRUPTER_THREAD the stub port
stopped opening entirely. 9021 was open (elfldr healthy) but nothing was
listening on 1234-1280, which is worse than the -DDEBUG-only build where
1234 did open.

start_interrupter_thread() is invoked before the listening socket is
bound, so its select() operates on an uninitialised gdb_socket and
startup fails before accept() ever runs. The hypothesis that the missing
interrupter thread was the reason packets went unserviced was wrong, or at
least enabling it is not the way to fix that.

Back to -DDEBUG only, which is the last configuration known to bring the
stub up.
